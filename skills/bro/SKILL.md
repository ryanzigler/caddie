# Bro

Restate your last message. Stop using jargon and speak coherently. Say it more simply and concisely, like one human talking to another.

Keep the facts and the conclusion identical. Don't soften a finding, don't add new claims, and don't retreat into a summary of what you did instead of the answer.

If `$ARGUMENTS` names a specific part of that message, restate only that part.
