# Mining brief

Paste the shared brief into every miner's prompt, followed by that miner's source section.

## Shared brief

Read-only voice analysis. Do not modify files outside the scratchpad, and never send, react,
comment, or post anywhere. You are gathering evidence about how Ryan Zigler writes, for a voice
profile a skill will apply later. Report observations as facts with counts or rates ("contractions
in 9 of 10 messages"), never as adjectives.

Contamination filter: anything Ryan may have pasted from an AI assistant is not his voice. Drop
items with a `Co-Authored-By: Claude` or `Generated by Claude Code` trailer, items referring to
Ryan in the third person, and items with generated structure (Summary or Test plan headings,
bold-lead-in bullet lists, groups of exactly three, em-dash-heavy prose). Report how many you
dropped.

Save the raw items you kept to the scratchpad path given in your source section so the main
thread can spot-check.

Report, under 900 words:

1. Coverage table: items kept, date range, filters applied, caps hit, anything unreachable.
2. Shape: typical length, how he opens, how he asks, how he pushes back, how he closes.
3. Mechanics: em dashes, ellipses, exclamation marks, parentheses, capitalization, emoji, code
   spans, bullets, bold, straight versus curly quotes, each with a rate.
4. Vocabulary: words and phrases he reaches for, with counts; hedges, intensifiers, fillers,
   profanity with the words and the moods they appear in; words a model uses that he never does.
5. Signature phrases with an observed rate ("about 1 in 12").
6. Tone when neutral, annoyed, pleased, joking, each with short evidence.
7. Ten verbatim excerpts under 45 words, scrubbed: replace every client, company, product, and
   person identifier with `[client]`, `[product]`, or `[name]`. Cro Metrics internal tools count
   as `[product]`. Generic technology names stay. Nothing personal from a DM.
8. Three generic-model-versus-Ryan pairs grounded in the evidence.

If your tools are unreachable, stop and report exactly what failed. Do not substitute another
source.
